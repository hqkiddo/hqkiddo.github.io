Privacy Policy — Illuminator

App: Illuminator (`com.hqkiddo.Illuminator`)
Effective date: July 28, 2026
Last updated: July 28, 2026

Illuminator is a home backyard control app. It sends commands to a gateway on your local network (for example pool and spa lights). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

What the app does

  • Connects over your local Wi‑Fi / LAN to a backyard gateway you configure (host, port, and API key stored in the app’s configuration on the device).
  • Reads device state from that gateway and sends control commands you choose (lights, bubblers, and similar backyard controls).

What we collect

Illuminator is designed to keep activity on your home network and device.

  • We do not create user accounts inside Illuminator.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or third‑party analytics trackers in the app.
  • We do not upload backyard commands or pool/spa state to Illuminator’s developers for advertising or profiling.
  • Configuration you enter (gateway address and API key) stays on your device / build configuration for talking to your gateway.

Data Apple may process

If you install via TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may process standard platform data under Apple’s own policies (for example crash reports you opt into, or App Store Connect analytics for the developer account holder). That processing is controlled by Apple and your device settings—not by a separate Illuminator cloud account.

Children

Illuminator is a household engineering / learning project. It is meant to be used with a parent or guardian who controls the Apple Developer / App Store Connect account and the home network gateway. It is not a social network and is not intended to collect children’s personal data.

Your choices

  • Uninstall the app to remove it from the device.
  • Change or remove gateway settings by rebuilding with updated configuration, or stop the gateway on your network.
  • Use device settings to limit analytics / crash sharing with Apple.

Contact

Privacy questions about Illuminator: github.com/hqkiddo/backyard-quest/issues (open an issue on the Illuminator / backyard-quest project), or contact the Apple Developer team account holder who publishes this app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after an update means you have read the revised policy.
